01About关于我

I am an AI Infra Research Engineer at Huawei Technologies, working on efficient large-model training and inference on large-scale accelerator clusters. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Michigan State University in 2025, and spent 2024 as a research graduate student at Los Alamos National Laboratory.

During my Ph.D. I worked on low-rank and tensor methods, control and optimization, and signal processing, imaging and statistics. Across all of it, I like problems where a clean mathematical structure — low rank, sparsity, the geometry of an optimizer — can be turned into an algorithm that runs faster and more reliably on real hardware; today I bring that toolkit to AI infrastructure.

我目前是华为技术有限公司的 AI Infra 研究工程师，致力于在大规模加速器集群上实现大模型的高效训练与推理。 2025 年我在密歇根州立大学获得应用数学博士学位，2024 年曾在洛斯阿拉莫斯国家实验室担任研究生研究员。

博士期间，我的研究方向是低秩与张量方法、控制与优化，以及信号处理、成像与统计。贯穿其中的，是我最喜欢的一类问题：把清晰的数学结构——低秩、稀疏、优化器的几何——转化为能在真实硬件上跑得更快、更稳的算法。如今我把这套工具用在 AI 基础设施上。
