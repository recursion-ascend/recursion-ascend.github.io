Who我是谁

I am an AI Infra Research Engineer at Huawei Technologies, working on efficient large-model training and inference on large-scale accelerator clusters. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Michigan State University in 2025, and spent 2024 as a research graduate student at Los Alamos National Laboratory.

During my Ph.D. I worked on low-rank and tensor methods, control and optimization, and signal processing, imaging and statistics. Across all of it, I like problems where a clean mathematical structure — low rank, sparsity, the geometry of an optimizer — can be turned into an algorithm that runs faster and more reliably on real hardware; today I bring that toolkit to AI infrastructure.

我目前是华为技术有限公司的 AI Infra 研究工程师，致力于在大规模加速器集群上实现大模型的高效训练与推理。 2025 年我在密歇根州立大学获得应用数学博士学位，2024 年曾在洛斯阿拉莫斯国家实验室担任研究生研究员。

博士期间，我的研究方向是低秩与张量方法、控制与优化，以及信号处理、成像与统计。贯穿其中的，是我最喜欢的一类问题：把清晰的数学结构——低秩、稀疏、优化器的几何——转化为能在真实硬件上跑得更快、更稳的算法。如今我把这套工具用在 AI 基础设施上。

Pr. 1例 1

How long does all-reduce take?all-reduce 要多久？

Given $p$ accelerators, a buffer of $n$ bytes on each, per-message latency $\alpha$ and link bandwidth $\beta$ — sum the buffers so that every accelerator ends up with the result. 设有 $p$ 个加速器，每个上有 $n$ 字节的数据，单次通信延迟为 $\alpha$，链路带宽为 $\beta$。要把所有数据求和，并让每个加速器都拿到结果。

(i)
Arrange them in a ring and cut each buffer into $p$ chunks of $n/p$ bytes. 把它们连成一个环，每份数据切成 $p$ 块，每块 $n/p$ 字节。
(ii)
Reduce-scatter: $p-1$ steps; each rank sends one chunk and adds the one it receives. Reduce-scatter：共 $p-1$ 步，每步每个节点发出一块、并累加收到的一块。 $$T_{\mathrm{RS}} = (p-1)\Big(\alpha + \frac{n}{p\,\beta}\Big)$$
(iii)
All-gather: another $p-1$ steps to circulate the finished chunks — same cost. All-gather：再用 $p-1$ 步把归约好的块传遍整个环，代价相同。
$$T_{\mathrm{ring}} = 2(p-1)\,\alpha \;+\; \frac{2(p-1)}{p}\cdot\frac{n}{\beta}$$

As $p\to\infty$ the bandwidth term tends to $2n/\beta$: it does not grow with $p$. The latency term does — which is why small messages prefer tree-like schedules such as recursive halving-doubling. 当 $p\to\infty$ 时，带宽项趋于 $2n/\beta$，不随 $p$ 增长；延迟项却会线性增长——所以小消息更适合递归减半/倍增这类树形算法。

∎
